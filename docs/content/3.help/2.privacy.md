Grok Desktop is a window around grok.com. It does not invent a second identity for you.

## Cookies and sign-in

When you sign in, grok.com stores cookies in **this app’s** WebKit data store. That is the same *kind* of storage Safari uses. It is **not** Safari’s cookie jar. Logging in here does not log in Safari. Logging in Safari does not log in here.

That is inconvenient. It is also how macOS apps are supposed to work.

## Camera and microphone

If you use Grok’s speech or video features, macOS will ask for the microphone or camera. The app only grants those requests for grok.com.

You can revoke access later in **System Settings → Privacy & Security**.

## What this app is not

This app is **not affiliated with xAI**. It does not send your chats to Kolja Nolte. It does not replace grok.com’s own privacy policy. Read that on grok.com if you care. You should.
