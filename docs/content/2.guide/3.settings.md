Open **Grok Desktop → Settings…** from the menu bar.

## Window

**Always on top** — the Grok window stays above other windows.

**Show usage stats** — a bar at the bottom with remaining usage from your grok.com session. No session, no useful numbers.

**Menu bar extra** — a small item in the menu bar. Click it to bring Grok Desktop forward.

## Display

**Safari user agent** — leave this on unless you know why you would turn it off. grok.com expects a Safari-class browser. This app *is* one.

**Zoom** — page size, from 75% to 200%.
