Sign-in happens on grok.com, in this app. There is no separate “Grok Desktop account.” There is your grok.com account.

## Sign in with xAI, Google, or Apple

On the grok.com page, choose **Sign in** and pick a method.

Complete every step **in Grok Desktop**. Email, password, account picker, consent, the “verifying your device” screen — all of it belongs here.

::warning
If Chrome or Safari opens and logs you in, that session lives in the browser. This app will not see it. Close the browser window, click **← Grok**, and sign in again inside Grok Desktop.
::

## Get back to Grok at any time

Whenever you leave grok.com, a **← Grok** bar appears at the top of the window.

Click it at any step of sign-in. It loads `https://grok.com` and drops the current login page. You are not trapped in Google’s maze. That is the point.

## After a successful sign-in

The app reloads grok.com so the home screen shows you as signed in. You should not need to press ⌘R.

If the page still looks logged out, press **⌘R** once. If that still fails, see [When things break](/help/when-things-break).

## Microphone and camera

Grok’s speech and video features may ask for the microphone or camera. macOS will prompt you. The app only allows those for grok.com.
