Grok Desktop is a native macOS app that opens [grok.com](https://grok.com) in a real window. It is written in Swift. It uses WebKit — the same engine Safari already has loaded — instead of bundling another copy of Chrome and calling it a product.

It is made by **Kolja Nolte**. It is **not affiliated with xAI**.

## What it is

You launch the app. You get grok.com. You sign in on the page. You talk to Grok. That is the entire pitch, and it is enough.

The window is still a macOS window: traffic lights, menus, shortcuts, find, zoom, print. It is not a browser. It will not grow tabs because someone on the internet once enjoyed having 47 of them.

## What you can do

- Open grok.com without opening a browser
- Sign in with xAI, Google, or Apple **inside this app**
- Pin the window always on top
- Show optional usage stats from your grok.com account
- Get back to grok.com from any sign-in page with **← Grok**

## When to use it

Use Grok Desktop when you want Grok on your Mac like a normal app, not like a bookmark you keep losing in a forest of tabs.

Use Safari when you want a browser. Use this when you do not.
